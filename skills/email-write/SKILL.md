  voice skill or memory. It wins over the workflow's defaults.

Expected input:
- Who the message is to and what it needs to say, or the message being replied to.

## The two rules that carry most of the value

**Give the reader facts, then ask for their judgment.** Tell them what they can't know from where
they sit and leave the professional call with them. Explaining an expert's own field to them reads
as a lecture, however politely it is put.

**Keep it short, with manners.** Under about 100 words: a pleasantry and specific thanks, one
clear ask with "please", their questions answered with exact facts, and thanks at the end.

Never send the message yourself unless the user asks.
